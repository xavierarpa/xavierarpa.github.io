About me

Xavier Arpa is a game designer & unity developer.

He has experience as a co-founder of two game studios, Bee Games and Boogysoft, where he worked on various mobile games.

He has also developed several indie games, some of which have been recognized in game jams and competitions.

He holds a Master's degree in Game Design and Game Development from EVAD.
